NUOVA — SETUP, IN THIS ORDER (about 15 minutes)
===============================================
Do steps 1 to 4 in the Firebase Console BEFORE uploading anything.
Firebase Console → https://console.firebase.google.com
Project: shri-jagdamba-monitor


STEP 1 — TURN ON EMAIL SIGN-IN (for you and Smita)
--------------------------------------------------
  Build → Authentication → Get started
  Sign-in method tab → Email/Password → Enable → Save


STEP 2 — TURN ON ANONYMOUS SIGN-IN (for customers)
--------------------------------------------------
  Same Sign-in method tab → Anonymous → Enable → Save

  This is what lets a customer submit an order without making an account,
  while still being blocked from reading anything of yours.


STEP 3 — CREATE YOUR TWO STAFF LOGINS
--------------------------------------------------
  Authentication → Users tab → Add user
    Email:    your email
    Password: pick a strong one
  Add user again for Smita.

  These two accounts are the ONLY way into the Order Management app.
  Write the passwords down somewhere safe — there is no other way in.


STEP 4 — PASTE THE SECURITY RULES  (THE IMPORTANT ONE)
--------------------------------------------------
  Build → Firestore Database → Rules tab
  Delete everything in the box.
  Open firestore.rules from this package, copy ALL of it, paste it in.
  Click PUBLISH.

  Read this bit carefully. Until you publish these rules, your database is
  almost certainly in test mode, which means ANYONE who opens the customer
  app and looks at its source can read your entire Firestore — every
  customer's name, phone number and address, every seed purchase, every
  cost and every sale. Publishing these rules closes that.

  After publishing:
    • Customers can create their own order and mark it paid. Nothing else.
    • Customers cannot read your records, your customer directory, or
      anybody else's order.
    • You and Smita, once signed in, have full access as before.


STEP 5 — UPLOAD THE UPDATED ORDER MANAGEMENT APP
--------------------------------------------------
  admin-index.html in this package is YOUR app with the login added.
  Put it wherever you currently open it from.
  If it lives in the NUOVASITE repo, upload it under the same filename
  it already has.

  First time you open it you will see the sign-in screen. Use the account
  from Step 3. There is a sign-out arrow at the top right.


STEP 6 — UPLOAD THE CUSTOMER APP
--------------------------------------------------
  Rename the  app  folder from this package to  app
  github.com → NUOVASITE → repo ROOT → Add file → Upload files
  Drag the  app  folder in → Commit.
  On your phone, close the Nuova app fully and reopen it.


STEP 7 — TEST IT END TO END
--------------------------------------------------
  1. Open the Order Management app on your laptop and sign in.
     Leave it open on the Records tab.
  2. On your phone, open the Nuova app and place a small test order —
     one 200 ml bottle to yourself.
  3. Within a second or two the order should appear in Records on the
     laptop, with a teal "App" badge.
  4. Tap the receipt icon on that row. The tax invoice should open with
     the right product name, rate and total.
  5. On the phone, choose "I have paid" and type any reference number.
     The badge in Records changes to "Payment claimed".
  6. In Records, mark it Delivered. Then delete the test order.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
--------------------------------------------------
"Enable Email/Password in Firebase Console"      → Step 1 not done
"No account with that email"                     → Step 3 not done
Order says "Not sent yet" on the phone           → Step 2 or Step 4 not done
Records stays empty after signing in             → rules not published,
                                                   or published with a typo;
                                                   re-paste the whole file

The customer app keeps working even if Firebase is unreachable — the order
is saved on the phone, shows "Not sent yet", and goes out automatically the
moment the connection is back. The WhatsApp message is still sent either
way, so an order can never be silently lost.
